Committee: Prof. Frank Dellaert (advisor), Prof. Matthew Gombolay, Prof. Danfei Xu

* Represented classical planning problems as factor graphs, extended the representation to combined task and motion planning and to concurrent plans, and tested it on the gripper domain from IPC 1998 and on centralized multi-robot planning.
* Introduced TableFactor, a discrete probabilistic factor optimized for sparsity. It addresses the slow inference caused by the exponential growth of the discrete state space, and multiplies sparse factors 19 times faster on average than GTSAM's existing discrete factor.
