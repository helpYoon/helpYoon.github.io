I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Texas at Austin, advised by [Prof. Peter Stone](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~pstone/) in the Learning Agents Research Group (LARG). I'm interested in task and motion planning and humanoid locomanipulation.

Before UT Austin, I received my M.S. and B.S. in Computer Science from the Georgia Institute of Technology. There I worked with Prof. Frank Dellaert in the BORG Lab on task planning in factor graph representations and contributed to the [GTSAM](https://github.com/borglab/gtsam) library.

Highlights
======
* Our paper on LLM-guided state estimation for partially observable task and motion planning will appear at **ICRA 2026**.
* Our team placed **2nd at RoboCup@Home 2025**.
