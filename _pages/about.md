I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Texas at Austin, advised by [Prof. Peter Stone](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~pstone/) in the Learning Agents Research Group (LARG). My research is on task and motion planning for robots, especially planning under partial observability with large language models and humanoid manipulation with learned controllers in the loop.

Before UT Austin, I received my M.S. and B.S. in Computer Science from the Georgia Institute of Technology. There I worked with Prof. Frank Dellaert in the BORG Lab on task planning in factor graph representations and contributed to the [GTSAM](https://github.com/borglab/gtsam) library.

Highlights
======
* Our paper on LLM-guided state estimation for partially observable task and motion planning will appear at **ICRA 2026**.
* Our team placed **2nd at RoboCup@Home 2025**. I lead a team of five graduate students building humanoid systems for household tasks.
